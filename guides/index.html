Bathroom renovation guides for Canberra homes

Guides to renovating a bathroom in Canberra, written from our own jobs: what it costs, how long it takes, and the decisions that are hard to undo.

They are about houses here: ex-government places and 1960s to 1980s brick homes with small bathrooms, slabs you must cut to move a fixture, winters that slow membrane cure times, and approvals that go to the ACT Government's planning authority, because there are no local councils in the ACT. Written by Oleg, owner of Apex Bathrooms ACT, renovating bathrooms here since 2008.

  • How to choose a renovator - licences, insurance, and the quote red flags.
  • The mistakes that cost the most - including the work that cannot legally be done yourself.
