How the designer works

Set the size of the room, then choose the tiles, the paint and the fixtures. You are picking from our own catalogue, not a generic list, so what you see is what we install verify, and the room builds itself in 3D as you go.

There is no price on screen. It is a design tool: it puts the room you actually want in front of us, and the quote comes from us in writing. In ex-government houses and 1960s to 1980s brick homes nobody knows what is under the floor until the room is stripped, so we look at the substrate before any quote is written. If you want a sense of the cost while you design, the bathroom renovation cost guide sets out the ranges by trade, and what a quote should include covers the rest.

What happens after you send it

Your design is emailed to you, so you keep a copy of the room you built. We receive it with the product list and the screenshots, call you to go through it, book a site inspection, and come back with a written, itemised quote - no ballpark figures and no hidden extras. We use your details to quote your job and nothing else - see our privacy policy.
