More about the business and how we work.

What a job looks like in your house

A bathroom is out of action for weeks, so how we work in your house matters as much as the finish. We keep a clean and safe worksite while the room is stripped, and we plan the job to finish on time and within the price you agreed.

When the floor comes up and the substrate under it has gone, you hear about it the same day, and you get the cost of putting it right in writing before we carry on. Nothing is tiled over and nothing is added to the bill without you agreeing to it first.

That is the whole reason the quote is itemised line by line: so you can see what changed and why verify.
